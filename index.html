NLS on the Lumpy Torus

Nonlinear Schrödinger evolution on a surface of revolution with metric ds² = dx² + A(x)²dθ², A(x)=√((1+cos²x)/2). Finite-element mass-conserving Laplace–Beltrami assembly, Crank–Nicolson time-stepping, Picard nonlinear solve with a convergence check. A Python companion to the lumpy-torus study. All 3-D frames are rendered on the metric-faithful crescent immersion.

01Geometry & curvature

Over exactly one period x∈[−π/2, π/2] (ends identified) the surface has exactly one elliptic closed geodesic (the belly, A=1, K=+0.5) and one hyperbolic one (the neck, A=0.707, K=−1).
